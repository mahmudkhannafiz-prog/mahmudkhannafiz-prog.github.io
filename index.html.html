Hello, I'm

Nafiz Mahmud Khan

I am a researcher in the Department of Business and Technology Management at the Islamic University of Technology (IUT), Gazipur, Bangladesh. My work sits where statistics, data and organisations meet: how statistics and data science are taught and learned, how management information systems support better decisions, and how information organisations adopt artificial intelligence responsibly.

Alongside education-focused questions, I collaborate on applied studies that put analytics to work — generative AI in digital marketing, human–AI collaboration among IT professionals, explainable machine learning for last-mile logistics in Dhaka, and sustainability analytics for green infrastructure investment. Across all of it, I care about making data methods understandable, transparent and useful to the people who rely on them.

When I'm not at my desk, I'm usually on a trail. I love to hike and roam around Bangladesh — from hills and forests to rivers and the coast — and those journeys keep me curious about the places and people behind the data.

What I study

Research Interests

My research asks how people learn to reason with data, and how organisations turn data and AI into better decisions.

Teaching and Learning of Statistics

How students build statistical thinking — reasoning about variability, uncertainty and evidence — and which teaching approaches, tools and assessments help them move beyond formulas toward real understanding.

Data Science Education

Designing learning experiences that combine statistics, computing and domain knowledge, so learners can work with real-world data, communicate findings clearly and use analytics responsibly.

Management Information Systems

How information systems and business analytics shape managerial decisions and operations, from logistics and finance to marketing — with a focus on transparent, explainable decision support.

AI Adoption in Information Organizations

Why and how information organisations adopt AI tools, how professionals collaborate with them in everyday work, and the ethical, governance and skills questions that adoption raises.

Selected work

Projects

Research projects I have led or contributed to, bringing analytics and AI to real organisational problems.

Logistics · Explainable AI

Explainable Analytics for Last-Mile Delivery in Dhaka

An XAI-driven framework that predicts delivery time from 30,000 orders and optimises multi-stop routes, using SHAP and LIME to keep model decisions transparent for logistics managers.

AI Adoption · Workplaces

Human–AI Collaboration in IT Workflows

A mixed-methods pilot study of IT professionals in Bangladesh exploring how tools like ChatGPT and Copilot are used day to day, and how professionals see AI's effect on productivity and careers.

Marketing · Generative AI

Generative AI in Digital Marketing

A function-based review mapping generative AI capabilities to specific marketing activities, alongside the risks of bias, hallucination and privacy and the governance they call for.

Sustainability · Finance

Investment-Grade Sustainability Analytics

A framework integrating Life Cycle Assessment with digital finance and ESG analytics to support data-driven investment decisions in green infrastructure projects.

Logistics · Economics

AI Investment and Logistics Performance

Cross-country evidence from the EU and MENA regions on whether investment in artificial intelligence translates into stronger national logistics performance.

Energy · Urban

Rooftop Solar in Dhaka's Homes

A survey-based study of the barriers and opportunities for rooftop solar adoption in Dhaka's residential sector, from upfront costs and roof space to incentives and awareness.
